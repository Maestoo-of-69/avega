# AVEGA — roadmap

Everything below is **planned and not implemented**. Nothing in the prototype depends on it.

1. **Not implemented** — Correlation of chat-reported symptoms and interview answers with psychological indicators over time.
2. **Not implemented** — A weekly short creative game that yields psychological indicators. The design and its scientific basis are to be chosen from published instruments before building.
3. **Not implemented** — Validated mood and anxiety scales (instruments to be taken from the full papers, see `docs/REFERENCES.md` N5).
4. **Not implemented** — HRV (needs beat-to-beat intervals from a sensor).
5. **Not implemented** — Radiation dose (needs a dosimeter source; no dose limits are shown until a real source exists, REFERENCES R2).
6. **Not implemented** — Prevention suggestions based on a person's own lead–lag links, once real data exist.
